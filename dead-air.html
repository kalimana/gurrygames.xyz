About this game

Dead Air is a short psychological horror game set in the booth of WTRY 88.1, a small AM station on the edge of a town that does not appear on most maps.

You are the overnight host. Between one and four in the morning, the lines light up with callers who know things they should not: the colour of your kitchen, the name of the next caller, the exact minute the power will fail. Every call is a piece of a morning you have not lived yet.

Play is built around the desk. Answer the line, cue the tape, log the call, and keep the show on air. The station is old and half-broken. The clock is not.

There is no combat. The pressure comes from what you choose to say, what you choose to play, and how long you leave a caller on hold. The wrong answer does not end the game. It changes what arrives at the door.

Dead Air is in development for Windows PC. The build is a single sitting, designed to be finished in one night with headphones on.

What to expect

  • A single-session story, 45–60 minutes
  • Analog broadcast puzzles
  • Callers who remember you
  • No combat, no chase sequences

Play with headphones. The station is mixed for them.

Content note

Psychological unease, implied violence, and sustained audio tension.

Development log

Notes from the booth

  1. 11 Nov 2026

    The station is on the air

    Dead Air is now listed. The booth is built, the call log is wired, and the first three stills are in the gallery. There is no download yet. When there is, it will appear here first.

  2. 28 Oct 2026

    Cutting the dead air

    The current build runs 52 minutes. We removed a second act that explained too much, and replaced it with one phone call that explains nothing. The show is better for it.

  3. 19 Sep 2026

    One room, one microphone

    We are keeping the camera close to the desk. No walk-and-talk, no monster in the hallway. The station is the whole world, and the callers are the weather.
