Development log

About the game

11 Nov 2026 / Dead Air

The station is on the air

Dead Air is now listed. The booth is built, the call log is wired, and the first three stills are in the gallery. There is no download yet. When there is, it will appear here first.

See the game page
  1. 28 Oct 2026

    Cutting the dead air

    The current build runs 52 minutes. We removed a second act that explained too much, and replaced it with one phone call that explains nothing. The show is better for it.

  2. 19 Sep 2026

    One room, one microphone

    We are keeping the camera close to the desk. No walk-and-talk, no monster in the hallway. The station is the whole world, and the callers are the weather.

All notes from the booth

The studio

Small on purpose.

Gurry Games is an independent horror studio. The catalogue is deliberately short, and a game only appears when it is ready to be looked at.

No teasers, no wishlists, no countdowns. When there is something to play, it will be here with a download, a trailer if there is one, and the files to check. Until then, the shelf stays honest.

  1. 01

    Finished before seen

    A game appears when it is ready, not when it is announced.

  2. 02

    No noise

    No countdown timers. No fake urgency. No account required.

  3. 03

    Keep the files

    Downloads stay up. Pages keep their links. The archive does not rot.
